# Vrinda Store Annual Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

An interactive Excel dashboard created to analyze Vrinda Store's annual sales performance and understand customer purchasing behavior, order status, and sales-channel performance.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze monthly sales and order trends
- Compare purchases by gender
- Understand order status distribution
- Identify top-performing states
- Analyze orders by age group and gender
- Compare sales channels
- Identify major product categories

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills

- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning
- Data Processing
- Excel Formulas
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers
- Data Visualization
- Dashboard Design

## 📊 Dashboard Features

- Orders vs. Sales by month
- Men vs. Women sales comparison
- Order status analysis
- Top 10 states by sales
- Age group vs. gender analysis
- Channel-wise order distribution
- Interactive filters for Month, Channel, and Category

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Women contributed approximately 64% of purchases.
- Men contributed approximately 36% of purchases.
- March recorded the highest sales and order activity.
- Approximately 92% of orders were delivered.
- Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh were among the top-performing states.
- Adults represented the largest share of orders in the age-group analysis.
- Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra were major sales channels.
- Amazon had the largest channel contribution in the dashboard.
